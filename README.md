# Sharpen

Training, logging and coaching. Built for Ben — six-day home gym, Mounjaro, fat off without muscle off.

It's a web app that installs onto your phone's home screen and works with no signal. No app store, no account, no monthly fee.

---

## Put it on your phone (about 5 minutes, once)

**1. Make the repo**

On github.com, click **+** (top right) → **New repository**.
- Name it `sharpen`
- Leave it Public (Pages is free on public repos)
- Don't tick "Add a README"
- **Create repository**

**2. Upload these files**

On the empty repo page, click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in everything from this folder, then **Commit changes**.

**3. Turn the website on**

**Settings** → **Pages** (left sidebar) → under *Build and deployment*:
- Source: **Deploy from a branch**
- Branch: **main**, folder: **/docs**
- **Save**

Wait a minute, refresh, and the page shows your link — it'll be `https://YOURNAME.github.io/sharpen/`.

**4. Install it**

Open that link on your phone in Chrome.
- **Android:** menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen** → **Install**
- **iPhone:** Share button → **Add to Home Screen**

It now opens full screen with its own icon, like any other app. Installing it also stops the phone clearing your data, so do this rather than just bookmarking it.

## Where your data is kept

Everything — your plan, every set, every weigh-in — is stored in the browser on your phone and nowhere else. No server, no account, nothing sent anywhere.

That means:
- Clearing your browsing data or website data wipes it.
- It doesn't sync between your phone and your laptop. Each device has its own log.
- **Coach → Save a backup** writes a `.json` file. Put it in Drive or Dropbox. **Restore a backup** brings it all back, including onto a new phone.

The app nudges you if it's been more than a fortnight since the last backup.

## What's in it

**Today** — a sunrise that rises as you tick off the day: session, protein, water, steps, desk breaks, core squeeze, evening stretch, bed by 8. Coaching line changes with the hour, jab day, travel days and missed sessions.

**Train** — the day's session, one exercise at a time, with an animated demo of every movement. Big +/− buttons for weight and reps, a rest timer after each set, and a suggested weight based on what you actually lifted last time. Lower-body days start with a back check that lightens the spine-loaded lifts, or swaps to a back-care session.

**Progress** — weight chart against your 95 kg goal, strength numbers, weekly protein and water averages, session history, and a copy-out for your Google Sheet.

**Library** — every exercise and stretch, animated, with cues. Plus **"Something's tight — what do I stretch?"**: tap the areas that are complaining and it builds the routine that reaches them.

**Coach** — your plan settings, your reasons, what the training is doing for your heart, blood sugar, back and circulation, travel days, a calendar reminders file, offline answers to the common questions, and backups.

## The plan is yours to change

**Coach → Change my plan.** Pick the days you can lift, how long you've got, whether cardio and stretching are folded into each session or get their own day, whether you want a walk on rest days, and whether to track body weight at all. The week rebuilds itself and your logged history stays exactly where it is.

## How it picks your weights

It remembers what you actually lifted and works from that, not from a plan on paper.

| Time since you last did that exercise | What it puts on the bar |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 10 days | Same weight, or a small step up if you hit every rep |
| 11–20 days | Exactly where you left off |
| 3–6 weeks | 10% lighter |
| 6 weeks or more | 20% lighter, to rebuild the movement first |

## Reminders

The app can't buzz your phone on its own — no web app can, reliably. **Coach → Make my reminders file** builds a calendar file instead. Open it, your phone adds the events, and your normal calendar alerts do the reminding from then on: training sessions, weekday desk resets, and the 7:45pm stretch.

## The live coach (optional)

The six answers under **Ask your coach** work offline. For an actual back-and-forth, add an Anthropic API key from console.anthropic.com under **Add an API key**. It's pay-as-you-go and costs cents a month. The key is stored on your phone only and goes straight to Anthropic — it never touches GitHub. It's left out of backup files on purpose.

## Changing the app itself

You need Node.js installed.

```bash
npm install      # once
npm run dev      # live preview while you edit src/App.jsx
npm run build    # writes docs/ — commit that folder for the site to update
```

Everything lives in `src/App.jsx`. The weekly program is generated by `buildDays()`, the exercise drawings are in `POSES`, and the sets/reps/starting weights are in `META`.

## Not medical advice

This is a training log with a coach's voice, not a clinician. Mounjaro doses and side effects, pain that lasts more than a week, and anything that runs down a leg or arm are for your GP or physio.
